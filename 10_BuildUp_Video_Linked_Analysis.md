Feature Research Document

BuildUp

Video-Linked Player Performance Analysis

Product

BuildUp

Selected Feature

Video-Linked Player Performance Analysis

Dataset Used

FIFA World Cup 2026 Player Performance Dataset

Purpose

Research the existing product feature and identify how the idea relates to the
FIFA tabular dataset.

Why this feature was selected

BuildUp connects structured football data to specific moments in match video. This is relevant to a FIFA
performance-analysis project because numerical player statistics can be paired with evidence from match events.

Feature Research

1. BuildUp states that its system turns filmed football matches into structured data, player profiles, scouting workflows
and video-linked insights.

2. Its products connect statistics, events, clips and reports to match video. The Team product includes player and
team analysis with events, heatmaps and tactical data.

3. Video-linked analysis is useful because a numerical statistic alone does not always show the context in which an
action occurred.

4. For the FIFA dataset, the project could conceptually connect a player's statistical record to video clips or event
evidence when such footage and event identifiers are available. The supplied tabular dataset alone does not provide
the video.

How it could relate to the FIFA dataset

Possible project implementation: show player statistics alongside linked event clips or match moments. If video is
unavailable, retain this as a future product feature rather than claiming it is present in the dataset.

Important distinction

This document describes the researched product feature and a possible adaptation to the user's FIFA dataset. It does
not claim that the FIFA dataset contains proprietary data from the named product, and it does not claim that the
project reproduces the product's proprietary algorithms.
